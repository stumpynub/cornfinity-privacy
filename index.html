Cornfinity Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Cornfinity is a single-player farming game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information the game and the advertising service inside it handle, and the choices you have.

Your game save stays on your device

Your farm, coins, progress, outfits and settings are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. We do not run a game server, we have no accounts or sign-in, and we never receive or upload your save. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes your save permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Cornfinity is free and supported only by optional rewarded video ads. You choose whether to watch one, for example to double your offline earnings or start a Harvest Frenzy. There are no forced ads and no in-app purchases.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • approximate location (derived from your IP address),
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads and the app.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, personalise ads. This data is encrypted in transit. Google's handling of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission before it can use your device's advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Children

Cornfinity is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
